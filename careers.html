How we hire

Four steps, usually inside two weeks

Intellivra Global runs the same hiring process we ask our clients to run: structured, scheduled quickly, and with feedback at every stage.

  1. Application review within 3 business days

    A recruiter reads every application. If your background does not match the requisition, you get a short note saying why rather than silence.

  2. Recruiter conversation, 30 minutes

    Role, compensation range, work authorisation and timeline — all covered on the first call so nobody wastes a week discovering a mismatch.

  3. Technical interview with a practising engineer

    A working session on a realistic problem. No trick puzzles, no whiteboard algorithms unrelated to the job.

  4. Offer within 48 hours of the final round

    Written offer with the full compensation breakdown, benefits and start date, plus time to consider it without pressure tactics.

Working here

What Intellivra Global offers

Benefits differ by country and by engagement type. These are the commitments that apply to every internal employee and every W2 consultant in the United States.

Health cover from day one

Medical, dental and vision with no waiting period, plus employer-funded coverage for dependants.

Paid time off that accrues on contract

W2 consultants accrue PTO and paid holidays, which is unusual in contract staffing and deliberate on our part.

Immigration support handled in-house

H-1B transfers, extensions and green card processing are managed and funded by us, not pushed back to you.

Certification budget

An annual budget per person for cloud, data and security certifications, including the exam fee and study time.

Bench pay between engagements

Consultants on our payroll are paid during short gaps between client engagements, up to the policy limit.

A recruiter who stays with you

The same recruiter handles your placement, your extension and your next role, rather than passing you around a desk.

Benefit details are confirmed in writing with every offer.
