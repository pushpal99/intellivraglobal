Choosing a model

Which engagement model fits the work?

The right model depends on three things: who manages the work day to day, where the budget sits, and how well the scope is defined.

Choose staff augmentation when

  • Your team manages the work and owns the backlog
  • Scope shifts sprint to sprint
  • You need capacity, not a delivery plan

Choose contract-to-hire when

  • Headcount is not approved yet, but the work is real
  • You want to validate fit before converting
  • You need someone starting in under two weeks

Choose a managed team when

  • The outcome can be written down and accepted
  • You do not have management bandwidth to spare
  • You want milestone pricing rather than hours
