Mission

Our mission is to make technical hiring fast, honest and verifiable

The average enterprise engineering role in the United States takes over a month to fill, and a large share of that time is spent reading resumes that were never a real match. We think that is a solvable problem, and that solving it does not require pretending an algorithm can judge an engineer.

So we do three things differently. We keep a continuously vetted bench instead of starting from scratch on every requisition. We put a practising engineer in every technical interview and write down what they found. And we publish our rates and our margin when asked, because a staffing relationship that depends on the client not knowing the numbers is not a relationship worth having.

Honest by default

Rate, margin and market data disclosed on request. If we cannot fill a role well, we say so in the intake call rather than three weeks later.

Engineers judge engineers

Every technical screen is run by someone who does the work. AI narrows the field; it never decides who is good.

Accountable to outcomes

Where scope can be written down, we would rather be paid for the result than for the hours it took.

The name

Why “Intellivra”

“Intellivra” joins intellectus, the Latin root behind intellect and understanding, with vara, a Sanskrit word meaning choice or excellence — the two halves of the business. The Latin half is the judgement we apply to a requisition; the Sanskrit half is the standard we hold candidates to, and a nod to the delivery team in {{ offices.1.locality }} who make up half the company.

“Global” is literal rather than aspirational: the company runs on two continents, and every client engagement is staffed from whichever side of that footprint fits the work, the budget and the time zone.
