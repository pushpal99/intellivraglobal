Why Intellivra

Three things typical staffing firms do not do

Intellivra Global differs from a traditional staffing agency in three measurable ways: shortlists arrive in 48 hours instead of two weeks, candidates are screened by engineers with AI-assisted matching rather than keyword search, and engagements can be priced on outcomes instead of hours.

Speed that is measured, not promised

Our recruiters work from a live bench of 4,000+ engineers who have already completed technical vetting. That is why the median approved-requisition-to-shortlist time is 48 hours, against an industry median of roughly two weeks.

AI-assisted vetting, human final say

Structured extraction ranks every applicant against the requisition, then a practising engineer runs the live interview and reviews a work sample. The model narrows the field; it never makes the hiring decision, and every rejection reason is recorded.

How an Intellivra engagement runs

  1. Intake call, same day

    A recruiter and a technical lead join a 30-minute intake to agree the must-have skills, the interview loop and the target rate before sourcing begins.

  2. Shortlist in 48 hours

    You receive three to five profiles with interview notes, a work-sample summary, rate, location and work authorisation on a single page.

  3. Interview and offer

    We coordinate scheduling, collect structured feedback and manage the offer, including rate negotiation and counter-offer risk.

  4. Onboarding and check-ins

    Background checks, equipment and access are tracked to a start date, then we check in at day 7, day 30 and each quarter.

FAQ

IT staffing questions, answered plainly

Engagement models, vetting, pricing and guarantees — the eight questions procurement and hiring managers ask us most often.

How fast can Intellivra Global submit candidates?

Intellivra Global submits three to five pre-vetted profiles within 48 hours of an approved job description. Every candidate has already completed a structured technical screen and a work-sample review, so client interviews usually start in the same week.

What engagement models does Intellivra Global offer?

Intellivra Global offers four engagement models: IT staff augmentation on an hourly bill rate, contract and contract-to-hire, direct placement on a percentage of first-year salary, and outcome-based managed teams delivered under a statement of work.

Most enterprises start with staff augmentation for a single role, then move to a managed team once the scope is well defined.

What is the difference between staff augmentation and a managed team?

In staff augmentation the client manages the engineer day to day and pays for time. In a managed team, Intellivra Global owns the delivery plan, the team composition and the agreed outcomes, and the client pays for milestones defined in a statement of work.

How does Intellivra Global vet engineers?

Every candidate passes a four-stage screen: an AI-assisted resume and skills match against the requisition, a live technical interview with a practising engineer, a work-sample or code review, and employment plus reference verification. Roughly one in fourteen applicants reaches a client submission.

Does Intellivra Global support W2, C2C and 1099 engagements?

Yes. Intellivra Global engages consultants on W2, corp-to-corp and 1099 terms in the United States, and supports H-1B transfers, E-Verify onboarding and OPT or CPT candidates where the role and client policy allow.

How is Intellivra Global priced?

Contract roles are billed at a transparent hourly rate with the margin disclosed on request. Direct placement is billed as a percentage of first-year base salary, typically 18 to 22 percent. Managed teams are priced per sprint or per milestone under a statement of work.

Is there a replacement guarantee?

Yes. Contract placements carry a 30-day replacement guarantee and direct placements carry a 90-day guarantee. If a placed professional leaves or is released for performance inside that window, Intellivra Global replaces them at no additional fee.

Can Intellivra Global work through our VMS or MSP programme?

Yes. Intellivra Global is set up to work as a subcontractor or tier-two supplier inside vendor management systems such as Fieldglass, Beeline and Coupa, and can also contract directly with procurement outside a managed service programme.
